Geographic Distribution
From April to June 2025, the regions with the highest reporting of newly diagnosed cases were NCR, CALABARZON (Region 4A), Central Luzon (Region 3),
Central Visayas (Region 7), SOCCSKSARGEN (Region 12), Western Visayas (Region 6), and Davao (Region 11), accounting for 74% (3,657) of the total cases,
while 26% of cases (1,281) were reported from other regions [Figure 4]®. Between June 2020 and June 2025, these same regions minus Region 12
contributed to 73% (55,696) of the total reported cases. The remaining 20,717 cases (27%) were distributed across other regions [Table 1]"°.
From 1984 to June 2025, NCR and Regions 4A, 3, 7, 11, and 6 consistently report the highest number of cases, with a total of 117,369 cases, representing 76%
of all reported cases [Table 1]. During this period, 35,168 cases (23%) were reported from other regions within the country, 13 cases (<1%) were reported
from overseas, and 1,233 cases (<1%) had no data on their region of residence.

Geographic Distribution
From October to December 2024, the regions with highest reporting of newly diagnosed cases were NCR, CALABARZON (Region 4A), Central Luzon
(Region 3), Western Visayas (Region 6), Central Visayas (Region 7), and Davao (Region 11), accounting for 72% of the total cases, while 1,157 cases (27%)
were reported from other regions [Figure 5] °. Between January 2019 and December 2024, these same regions contributed to 77% or 60,587 of the total
reported cases. The remaining 19,165 cases (23%) were distributed across other regions [Table 1]"°.
Similarly, from January 1984 to December 2024, these regions have consistently reported the highest number of cases, with a total of 114,231 cases,
representing 79% of all reported cases [Table 1]. During this period, 28,341 cases (20%) were reported from other regions within the country, 11 cases
(<1%) were reported from overseas, and 1,195 cases (1%) had no data on their region of residence.

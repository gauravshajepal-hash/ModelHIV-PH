What are the policies and 
guidelines supporting HIV 
/ STI surveillance?
The Epidemiology Bureau shall maintain a comprehensive HIV and AIDS monitoring and 
evaluation program that shall serve the following purposes:
(a) Determine and monitor the magnitude and progression of HIV and AIDS in the 
Philippines and regularly provide a list of priority areas with high magnitude of HIV 
and AIDS cases and co-infections to help the PNAC evaluate the adequacy and 
efficacy of HIV prevention and treatment programs being employed;
(b) Receive, collate, process, and evaluate all HIV-and-AIDS-related medical reports from 
all hospitals, clinics, laboratories and testing centers, including HIV-related deaths and 
relevant data from public and private hospitals, clinics, laboratories and testing centers, 
including HIV-related deaths and relevant data from public and private hospitals, 
various databanks or information systems; Provided, That it shall adopt a coding system 
that ensures anonymity and confidentiality; and
(c) Submit, through its Secretariat, quarterly and annual reports to the PNAC containing 
the findings of its monitoring and evaluation activities in compliance with this mandate

What is IHBSS?
• Integrated HIV behavioral and Serologic Surveillance (IHBSS) is a routine HIV 
survey among key populations
• Has 2 components: behavioral (Interview) , and serologic (blood tests)
• Conducted every 3-4 years with the following objectives:
• Determine prevalence of HIV and other STI among Key populations
• Determine behavioral factors associated with STI/HIV transmission
• Determine outcome of HIV interventions
• Provide strategic information to guide policies, programs, and services
• Conducted among high burden cities (Category A)
• Employs different sampling method per key populations
Importance of IHBSS
• Provides data for planning and development of policies (national and site specific)
• Development of estimates (Key populations and PLHIV)
• Monitoring and Evaluation of HIV interventions

What is new with 2022 
IHBSS?
• New sampling 
methodology
• Flexible data collection 
arrangement 
• Blood screening through 
finger prick
• Electronic data 
collection 
Key populations
• Men having Sex with Men, & Transgender Women
• Freelance Female Sex Worker
• Person who Inject Drugs
Sampling methodology
• Respondent Driven Sampling (RDS) will be utilized for all Key 
populations.
• For MSM & TGW , a flexible data collection method will be used 
similar to Community Based Screening (CBS) to ensure barriers on 
willingness to join the survey will be dismantled. 
Serologic component
• MSM & TGW,
• screening through finger prick
• HIV, hepatitis B, & syphilis
• FFSW & PWID, blood draw by a medical technologist
• HIV, hepatitis  B, syphilis , and hepatitis C (PWID)
Survey administration
• Data will be collected electronically using ODK
• Interview will be conducted in person (face to face)
• Automatic validation of responses will be integrated into the application to improve accuracy

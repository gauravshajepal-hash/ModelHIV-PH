2025 HIV Estimates Updates Inputs:
Concept Review of Programmatic Coverages
Three (3) Main Inputs:
1. Coverage - % of people on PrEP, specifically % of person risk 
years where a person is on PrEP
2. Effective Use - refers to the appropriate use of PrEP during 
periods of HIV risk to achieve high levels of protection against HIV 
acquisition
3. Efficacy - how well PrEP performs under ideal and controlled 
circumstances
